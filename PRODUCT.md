# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary: B2B calling agents at Lone Star Legacy who call U.S. businesses during U.S. business hours, which is a night shift in Pakistan. They work in a dim office on desktop browsers, next to VC Dialer, with Legacy Flow open for the entire shift. On any screen their job is the call loop: get the next lead, call, record the outcome, schedule the callback, repeat.

Secondary: one Management user (lead imports, request approvals, live monitoring, reports, performance review), one Admin (users, permissions, dispositions, fields, settings) and one HR user (employee records, documents, attendance, leave). They check in through the shift and at period end, usually on the same night schedule.

## Product Purpose

Legacy Flow is Lone Star Legacy's calling CRM. It imports lead spreadsheets with any column layout, catches duplicates and bad phone formats before import, distributes leads with a 5-minute approval window and hard locking, gives agents a fast calling workspace, and gives Management full visibility of calls, activity and performance.

Success looks like: agents spend their time on calls rather than on the tool; Management trusts every number because it traces back to recorded events; no lead is ever assigned to two agents or silently lost.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, premium. The quiet black-and-gray surfaces carry the Lone Star Legacy identity; color is reserved for status (call outcomes, due callbacks, warnings). The voice is plain and operational: "Request leads", "Save outcome", "Approve 30 leads". Never cute, never salesy.

## Anti-references

- Old call-center software: cluttered gray forms, tiny fonts, Windows-era chrome, every field visible at once.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: gradients, giant hero stat cards, purple accents, decorative charts that carry no decision.
- Playful consumer apps: bright palettes, bubbly shapes, emoji, confetti.

## Design Principles

1. **The next call is one action away.** Agent screens are optimized for the call loop; everything else is secondary and out of the way.
2. **Quiet until it matters.** Neutral surfaces by default. Color and motion appear only to signal state: a callback coming due, a request waiting for approval, a do-not-call warning.
3. **Numbers you can trust.** Show raw data and where it came from. Every metric traces to recorded events; nothing is decorative.
4. **Built for eight hours at night.** Comfortable contrast in the dark theme, no glare, stable layouts that do not jump, fully keyboard-operable.
5. **Each role sees its own job.** Agents never see management-only metrics; every role gets a focused surface rather than one screen with everything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA in both themes: 4.5:1 for body text and placeholders, 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries, visible focus rings, full keyboard operation of the call loop, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, and status never conveyed by color alone (always paired with an icon or label).
